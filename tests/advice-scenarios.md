# Manual advice review

These worked scenarios review the skill's diagnostic path against the pinned handbook. They are an author review, not an independent agent evaluation or evidence from actual training runs. No GPU jobs were launched.

| Request | Advice reached through the skill | Evidence and acceptance check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “Aux loss is 1.0, but one GPU is slow. Should I turn off balancing?” | The loss alone cannot establish balance. Collect per-layer expert and device counts, retain zero-count experts, and compare micro-batch with global-batch MaxVio. Change balancing or placement only if the count distribution supports it. | [Cost of imbalance](../src/chapters/3-anatomy-of-moe-training.md#s-3-3-cost-of-imbalance), [measurement scope](../src/chapters/A-appendix.md#s-a-1-router-stats). Acceptance: distinguish flat probabilities from equal token counts; `[2,2,4,0]` gives MaxVio 1. |
| “Multi-node training became much slower; NCCL reports Socket.” | Verify the intended network/container transport, then benchmark all-to-all at the actual allocation size. If transport and balance are sound, profile exposed dispatch/combine and consider placement, fan-out or compatible overlap. | [Interconnect](../src/chapters/3-anatomy-of-moe-training.md#s-3-7-interconnect), [profiling](../src/chapters/3-anatomy-of-moe-training.md#s-3-7-profiling). Acceptance: do not transfer the source's approximately 25× slowdown as a prediction or treat all-reduce as sufficient validation. |
| “One rank OOMs, but peak memory on the dashboard looks safe.” | Reset and read peak allocated memory on every rank over the same interval; inspect the failing rank's load. Address imbalance if demonstrated, then evaluate memory interventions against their overhead and overlap constraints. | [Memory](../src/chapters/3-anatomy-of-moe-training.md#s-3-7-memory), [run health](../src/chapters/A-appendix.md#s-a-1-run-health). Acceptance: recognize rank-0 logging can omit the failing rank; do not assume the average or dashboard peak is global. |
| “NaN appeared with flat grad norm, parameter norm and z-loss.” | Locate the failing operation and use the source's rerun procedure on another GPU to distinguish hardware-specific from reproducible computation. Flat preceding metrics do not prove healthy computation. Do not disable checks or prescribe a router-loss change without evidence. | [Stability](../src/chapters/3-anatomy-of-moe-training.md#s-3-6-stability). Acceptance: a second failure on the same GPU does not settle the diagnosis; keep hypotheses distinct from confirmed causes. |
| “Use this Megatron balancing recipe in another framework.” | Explain the intended balancing mechanism and scope, inspect the target implementation, and verify equivalents in its installed revision or official documentation. Until verified, provide conceptual changes and identify the missing mapping. | [Load balancing](../src/chapters/3-anatomy-of-moe-training.md#s-3-3), [monitoring](../src/chapters/A-appendix.md#s-a-1). Acceptance: no invented flags, no promise of the source's measured gains, and no job launch implied by a request for advice. |

The review also checked that source-specific alarm thresholds in [failure signals](../src/figures/moe-failure-signals.md) remain illustrative or attributed, rather than becoming universal skill thresholds.
